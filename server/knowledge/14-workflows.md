# Common Workflows — Guide

Everyday situations and the screens that handle them.

## A customer phones to book

1. Click **New Appointment** on Appointments (`/appointments/new`).
2. Search for the caller by name or phone under **Customer**, or click **New customer** and enter their name and phone number.
3. Choose the service, the date and one of the free times.
4. Click **Save Appointment** and tell the caller the time.

If the caller gave an email address, they get the confirmation with a link to manage the booking.

## A staff member is sick today

1. Open **Availability** (`/availability`) and choose the person in **Showing**.
2. Click **Add Exception**, choose **Block full day** for today and click **Add Exception**.
3. Open each appointment listed under **future appointments outside the current availability**.
4. Click **Reschedule** to move it to someone else's free time or another day, or **Cancel** it.
5. Call or message the customers whose appointments changed.

No one can book that person while the exception is in place.

## Closing for a public holiday

1. Open **Availability** and choose **Business hours** in **Showing**.
2. Click **Add Exception**, choose **Block full day**, pick the date and type the **Reason**, for example Public holiday.
3. Click **Add Exception**.
4. Reschedule or cancel any appointment listed as outside the current availability.

## A customer did not turn up

After the start time, open the appointment and click **Mark No-show**. It stays in the customer's history as No-show.

## A customer is running late or arrives early

Nothing has to change in the app. If the appointment must move, click **Reschedule** on it and choose a free time; the customer gets a reschedule update.

## A customer wants to change their booking

Customers reschedule or cancel themselves with the link in their confirmation email, until the cutoff set in **Settings → Public Booking**. After the cutoff they see your contact details and call you: open the appointment and click **Reschedule** or **Cancel**.

## Starting the day

Open **Appointments**: the **Today** tab lists today's appointments in time order, and the bell in the top bar shows today's appointments too. Mark each one **Complete** or **Mark No-show** after it starts.

## Taking on a new team member

1. Add them in **Settings → Team** with **Add Staff** and tick their **Assigned services**.
2. Set their working hours in **Availability**.
3. If they should sign in, click **Login access**, choose **Staff** and click **Send invitation**.
