# Services — Guide

Services are what customers can book, such as Consultation or Haircut. Each service has a name and a duration. They are managed in **Settings → Services** (`/settings/services`). There are no prices, categories or service photos: customers pay the business directly.

## Adding a service

1. Go to **Settings → Services** (`/settings/services`).
2. Click **Add Service**.
3. Enter the **Service name**.
4. Enter the **Duration (minutes)**, or pick one of the **Common durations**. Use whole minutes between 5 and 1440.
5. Leave **Active** on so it can be booked.
6. Click **Save**.

A new service is offered by every active team member. Choose who offers it in **Settings → Team** with **Assigned services**.

## Editing a service

1. Go to **Settings → Services** (`/settings/services`).
2. Click **Edit** next to the service.
3. Change the **Service name**, **Duration (minutes)** or **Active**.
4. Click **Save**.

Changing the duration applies to new bookings only. Existing appointments keep their booked time and duration.

## Deactivating a service

1. Go to **Settings → Services** (`/settings/services`).
2. Click **Deactivate** next to the service.

An inactive service is hidden from the booking link and the New Appointment form. Its existing appointments are kept. Click **Activate** to offer it again.

## Choosing who offers a service

Who offers a service is set on each team member: in **Settings → Team**, click **Edit** on the person and tick their **Assigned services**. When only one person offers a service, customers are not asked to choose a provider.

## Service duration and booking times

Appointments last exactly the service's duration. The free times offered to customers start on a regular grid within working hours, and a time is offered only when the whole appointment fits before a break, a blocked period or the end of the day.

## Who can manage services

The owner and managers can add, edit and deactivate services. Staff cannot open Settings → Services.
