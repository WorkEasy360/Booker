# Reports & Analytics — Complete Guide

## What are reports?

The Reports section (`/reports`) shows analytics and performance metrics for your business based on appointment data.

## Accessing reports

Go to **Reports** (`/reports`) from the left sidebar.

## Date range selection

All reports are filtered by a date range. Options:
- **Last 7 days** — the past week
- **Last 30 days** — the past month (default)
- **Last 3 months** — the past quarter
- **Last year** — the past 12 months

Select the range using the buttons at the top of the page.

## Summary metrics (6 stat cards)

| Metric | Description |
|---|---|
| Total Appointments | Count of all appointments in the selected period |
| Total Revenue | Sum of prices from confirmed + completed appointments only |
| Avg per Day | Total appointments divided by number of days in the range |
| Cancellation Rate | Percentage of appointments that were cancelled |
| No-show Rate | Percentage of appointments marked as no-show |
| Completion Rate | Percentage of appointments marked as completed |

Note: Revenue is counted only from appointments with status "confirmed" or "completed". Cancelled and no-show appointments are excluded from revenue totals.

## Booking Volume Chart

A line/area chart showing bookings over time. Displays two data series:
- **Bookings** (green) — total appointments per time bucket
- **Cancellations** (red) — cancelled appointments per time bucket

Time buckets are automatically calculated (up to 30 data points across the selected range).

## Status Breakdown (Pie Chart)

Shows the distribution of appointments by status:
- Scheduled
- Confirmed
- Completed
- Cancelled
- No-show

## Service Performance Table

A table showing which services are most popular:
- Service name
- Number of bookings (with a relative progress bar)
- Revenue generated by that service

Sorted by bookings, descending.

## Staff Workload Chart

A bar chart showing the number of appointments per staff member. Helps identify your busiest team members.

## Top Customers Table

Shows the top 5 customers by total booking count:
- Customer name and email
- Total bookings
- Last booking date

## Exporting reports

The **Export** button is available but currently shows: "Connect an export integration in Settings to download reports." Full CSV/PDF export is a planned feature.

## Reports API endpoints

| Endpoint | Query Params | Returns |
|---|---|---|
| GET /api/reports/summary | range (7d, 30d, 3m, 1y) | totalAppointments, revenue, avgPerDay, cancellationRate, noShowRate, completionRate |
| GET /api/reports/by-service | range | Array of service bookings/revenue |
| GET /api/reports/by-staff | range | Array of staff booking counts |
| GET /api/reports/top-customers | — | Top 10 customers by booking count |

## Understanding the metrics

**Why is my revenue lower than expected?**
Revenue only counts appointments with status "confirmed" or "completed". Make sure to mark appointments as confirmed or completed after they take place. Appointments left in "scheduled" status are not counted in revenue.

**Why is the cancellation rate high?**
This shows the percentage of all appointments in the period that were cancelled. A rate above 20-30% is worth investigating. Check if your minimum notice or cancellation policy settings need adjustment.

**My chart shows no data**
If no appointments exist in the selected date range, charts will be empty. Try selecting a wider range (e.g., Last year).
