# Known Limitations — Guide

WorkEasy360 Booking is simple appointment scheduling. These features are not available in it. Some earlier versions had screens for them; they were removed.

## Online payments (Stripe, Razorpay, PayPal) and prices

Customers cannot pay online, and online payments are not available. There is no payment gateway such as Stripe, Razorpay or PayPal, no deposits, no invoices and no prices on services. Customers pay the business directly, and the booking page says so.

## Reports, charts and revenue

Reports, charts, analytics and revenue figures are not available, so there is no revenue per day, week or month. **Appointments** shows Today's Appointments, Upcoming, Completed this week, Cancelled this week and Total Customers. Each customer's history is on their page in **Customers**.

## Workspaces, branches and multiple locations

Workspaces, branches and several locations in one business are not available. A business has one timezone and one appointment location in **Settings → Business Details**. A separate business can be its own WorkEasy360 organization, chosen with the organization selector.

## Rooms, equipment and resources

Booking rooms, equipment or other resources is not available. Only staff members are booked, and each can have one appointment at a time.

## Several booking pages, themes and custom domains

There is one booking link per business. Separate booking pages per service or per staff member, page themes, custom colours and your own web domain are not available.

## Zoom and video meetings

Video meetings are not created automatically: the Zoom connector is coming soon and Google Meet is not available. For online appointments, write how you will send the video link in **Appointment location or online instructions** in **Settings → Business Details**.

## Google Calendar and Outlook sync

Syncing appointments to Google Calendar or Outlook is not available yet; the Google Calendar connector is coming soon. Customers can add their own booking to Google Calendar, Outlook or Apple Calendar from the confirmation page.

## Recurring and group bookings

Repeating appointments, classes and group bookings are not available. Each appointment is one customer, one service and one staff member.

## Waiting lists

A waiting list for full days is not available.

## Several reminders and other reminder times

Only one reminder, 24 hours before, is available. Other reminder times and second reminders are not available.

## Message templates

Editing the wording of emails and messages is not available. They use fixed wording with your business details.

## Customer tags, notes and custom fields

Customer tags, customer notes, addresses and custom booking questions are not available. Use the internal note on an appointment for private notes.

## Importing or exporting customers

Bulk import of customers from a spreadsheet and exporting data are not available. Customers are added one at a time or when they book online.

## Booking rules: minimum notice, buffers and the booking window

Minimum notice before a booking, buffer time between appointments and how far ahead customers can book cannot be changed. Customers can book up to 30 days ahead.

## Deleting an account, a business or customers

Deleting your account, your business or customer records yourself is not available. Ask WorkEasy360 support with **Help → Create Ticket**.

## AI chat for customers

The AI Assistant is for the business team inside the app. Customers on the booking page have no chat.
