# Customers — Guide

Customers (`/customers`) lists everyone who has booked or been added, with their phone, email, number of bookings and last booking. Customers are added automatically when they book online, and you can add them yourself.

A customer needs a name and at least one way to reach them: a phone number or an email address. Booking updates are emailed only when an email address is given.

## Adding a customer

1. Go to **Customers** (`/customers`) and click **Add Customer**.
2. Enter the customer's **Name**.
3. Enter a **Phone** number, an **Email** address, or both. At least one is required.
4. Click **Add Customer**.

If a customer with the same phone number or email address already exists, the form says so and offers **Use this customer** or **Open customer** instead of creating a duplicate.

## Adding a customer while booking

1. In **New Appointment**, under **Customer**, type in **Find customer by name, phone or email**.
2. If they are not found, click **New customer**.
3. Enter the name and a phone number or an email address, then click **Add Customer**.

## Finding a customer

1. Go to **Customers** (`/customers`).
2. Type in **Search by name, phone or email**. Phone numbers match with or without spaces and the country code.
3. Click the customer to open their details.

The search in the top bar also finds customers and appointments by name or phone.

## Viewing a customer's appointment history

Open the customer from **Customers**. **Contact Details** shows the name, phone and email. **Appointment History** lists their **Upcoming** appointments and their **Past and cancelled** ones, with date, time, service, staff member and status. Click an appointment to open it.

## Editing a customer's contact details

1. Open the customer and click **Edit Contact Details**.
2. Change the name, phone or email.
3. Click **Save Changes**.

Their appointments stay linked to the same customer.

## Booking an appointment for an existing customer

Open the customer and click **Book Appointment**. The New Appointment form opens with the customer already chosen.

## Customers without an email address

A customer with only a phone number can be booked normally. They do not receive booking emails; they get SMS or WhatsApp updates only when those channels are on and they agreed to them. The app shows **No email address: booking confirmations and reminders cannot be emailed to this customer.**

## Duplicate customers

The same email address cannot belong to two customers. A phone number already used by another customer is flagged, and you can open that customer instead.

An online booking made with an email address already on file is added to that customer. A phone number alone is matched only to a customer who has no email address. Online bookings never change an existing customer's details.

## Deleting a customer

Customers cannot be deleted in WorkEasy360 Booking, because their appointment history must stay complete. Correct their details with **Edit Contact Details** instead.
