# Frequently Asked Questions

## Do customers need an account to book?

No. Customers book through your booking link with their name and a phone number or an email address. They never create an account or sign in.

## Do customers pay anything to book?

No. Customers are never charged for booking. Online payment is not available: customers pay the business directly. SMS and WhatsApp messages are charged to the business's Consumption Wallet, never to the customer.

## Is there a mobile app?

There is nothing to install. WorkEasy360 Booking works in the browser on phones, tablets and computers, and customers book from any phone browser.

## Which languages are supported?

English and Hindi. Each person chooses their language in **My Account**, and customers choose theirs on the booking page.

## What is a booking reference?

Each appointment gets a reference such as WE-1A2B3C4D. It is shown on the appointment, the confirmation page and the emails. Customers use it with their email address to find their booking.

## Can two customers book the same time?

Not with the same staff member: a person can never be double-booked. Two different staff members can each have an appointment at the same time.

## Can a customer book several services in one appointment?

No. Each appointment is one customer, one service and one staff member at one time. Book a second appointment for another service.

## Where did the Dashboard, Calendar and Reports go?

WorkEasy360 Booking has four menus: Appointments, Availability, Customers and Settings. Today's figures are at the top of **Appointments**, and the calendar is the **Calendar** view of Appointments. Reports are not available.

## Is my business data shared with other businesses?

No. Each organization's appointments, customers and settings are kept separate. If you run more than one business, switch between them with the organization selector at the top left.

## Can staff see all customers?

No. Staff see only their own appointments. The owner and managers see every appointment and the Customers list.

## Are appointments changed when I edit hours or block a date?

No, nothing changes automatically. Availability lists the future appointments that no longer fit, so you can reschedule or cancel them and tell the customers.
