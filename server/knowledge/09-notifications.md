# Notifications — Guide

Notifications (`/settings/notifications`) decides how customers hear about their bookings. Customers get a booking confirmation, a reschedule update and a cancellation update, each with their secure manage-booking link, plus an optional reminder 24 hours before. Appointments are always saved first: if a message cannot be sent, the appointment is kept and the failure is shown on its details.

## Channels: email, WhatsApp and SMS

- **Email** (Default): sent when the customer gave an email address.
- **WhatsApp** and **SMS**: paid channels that use your WorkEasy360 shared connection. Each message is charged to your organization's Consumption Wallet. They can be switched on only when they show **Connected**; otherwise they show **Not connected — uses your WorkEasy360 shared connection when available.**

Customers are never charged for booking. An SMS or WhatsApp message goes only to customers with a valid phone number who agreed to that channel, for example by ticking the box when booking online.

## Sending appointment reminders by email, SMS or WhatsApp

The reminder goes 24 hours before each appointment by email, and also by SMS or WhatsApp when those channels are on. To set it up:

1. Go to **Settings → Notifications** (`/settings/notifications`).
2. Switch on **Reminder 24 hours before**.
3. Click **Save Changes**.

It is skipped for bookings made less than 24 hours ahead. Rescheduling replaces the reminder; cancelling stops it. Other reminder times and several reminders are not available.

## Turning on SMS or WhatsApp messages

1. Go to **Settings → Notifications** (`/settings/notifications`).
2. Under **Channels**, switch on **SMS** or **WhatsApp**. They can be switched on only when they show **Connected**.
3. Read the paid-channel note and click **Turn on**.
4. Click **Save Changes**.

SMS and WhatsApp messages are sent only after the business's WorkEasy360 shared connection is available, and each message is charged to the Consumption Wallet. If the balance is too low, the message is skipped and the appointment is kept. Customers can reply STOP to stop the messages.

## Turning off booking emails

1. Go to **Settings → Notifications** (`/settings/notifications`).
2. Switch off **Email** under **Channels**.
3. Click **Save Changes**.

Customers then get no confirmation, reschedule or cancellation email, including the manage-booking link, so they cannot change their booking online.

## Resending a confirmation email

1. Open the appointment.
2. In **Customer Emails**, click **Resend confirmation** (or **Resend cancellation** for a cancelled appointment).

Check the customer's email address first: correct it with **Edit Contact Details** on the customer if needed.

## Checking whether a message was sent

Open the appointment. **Customer Emails** shows each booking email as sent or not sent, with a button to resend the confirmation or cancellation. **Notifications** shows SMS and WhatsApp messages, with **Retry** for failed ones.

## The bell in the top bar

The bell shows today's appointments. A red dot shows until you open it. It is for you and your team, not a message to customers.

## Message wording and templates

Booking messages use fixed wording with your business name, the appointment details, the timezone, the location and the manage-booking link. A template editor is not available.
