# Calendar — Complete Guide

## What is the Calendar?

The Calendar (`/calendar`) provides a visual view of all appointments across day, week, and month views. It is a read-only overview — use the Appointments section to create or modify appointments.

## Accessing the calendar

Click **Calendar** in the left sidebar, or go to `/calendar`.

## Calendar views

The calendar supports three views:
- **Day** — shows all appointments for a single day in hour-by-hour format
- **Week** — shows the current week with appointments laid out by time
- **Month** — shows the entire month with appointments as event chips

## Navigation

- Use the **Previous** and **Next** arrow buttons to move backward/forward in time
- Click **Today** to jump to the current date
- Click on a day number in week/month view to jump to that day's detail view

## What appointments show on the calendar

All appointments for your organization are shown. Each appointment is represented as a colored block using the service's or staff member's assigned color. The appointment title typically shows the customer name and service.

## Appointment statuses in the calendar

Appointments are color-coded or labeled by status:
- **Scheduled** — normal appointment
- **Confirmed** — confirmed appointment
- **Completed** — past appointments that were completed
- **Cancelled** — shown with strikethrough or greyed out
- **No-show** — shown with a special marker

## My Space vs Calendar

- **My Space** (`/my-space`) shows your personal schedule — appointments assigned to you specifically
- **Calendar** (`/calendar`) shows all appointments across the entire organization

Use My Space when you want to see only your own bookings. Use Calendar for a team-wide overview.

## Navigating to an appointment

Click on any appointment in the calendar to open its detail page where you can view, edit, reschedule, or cancel it.

## Filtering the calendar

The calendar can be filtered by staff member, workspace, or service to focus on specific bookings. Use the filter controls at the top of the calendar view.
