# Team — Guide

The team is the people who take appointments (staff members, also called providers). They are managed in **Settings → Team** (`/settings/team`). You can take bookings yourself; add staff only when someone else takes appointments. Customers choose a provider only when more than one person can do the service.

## Adding a staff member

1. Go to **Settings → Team** (`/settings/team`).
2. Click **Add Staff**.
3. Enter the **Name** and **Email**, and optionally a **Phone** number.
4. Under **Assigned services**, tick the services this person offers.
5. Leave **Active** on.
6. Click **Save**.

The new team member starts with Monday to Friday, 09:00 to 17:00. Set their real hours in **Availability**. A team member without assigned services shows **No services assigned — cannot be booked yet**.

## Editing a staff member

1. Go to **Settings → Team** (`/settings/team`).
2. Click **Edit** next to the person.
3. Change the name, email, phone, **Photo** or **Assigned services**.
4. Click **Save**.

## Setting staff working hours and time off

Working hours, breaks and time off are not set in Team. They are set in **Availability** (`/availability`): choose the person in **Showing**, change their **Working Hours** and click **Save Working Hours**, or click **Add Exception** for time off.

## Inviting a staff member to sign in

1. Go to **Settings → Team** (`/settings/team`).
2. Click **Login access** next to the person.
3. In **Sign-in role**, choose **Staff — sees only their own appointments** or **Owner — manages the business**.
4. Click **Send invitation**.

The person gets an email with a link to set their password. Their status changes from **No login** to **Invited**, then to **Can log in** once they accept. **Invitation history** lists the invitations sent. The person must be Active to be invited.

## Turning off a staff member's sign-in

1. Click **Login access** next to the person.
2. Click **Turn off sign-in**.

Their status shows **Login off** and they cannot sign in. Click **Turn sign-in back on** to give access back. Use **Reset two-factor sign-in** when someone has lost their authenticator app.

## Making a staff member inactive (deactivating)

1. In **Settings → Team**, click **Edit** next to the person.
2. Switch **Active** off and click **Save**.

Inactive members cannot be booked. Their appointments are kept. Turn **Active** back on to make them bookable again.

## Who can manage the team

Only the owner can add, edit, deactivate and invite team members.
