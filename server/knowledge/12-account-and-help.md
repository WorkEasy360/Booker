# My Account — Guide

My Account (`/account`) holds your own profile, password, two-factor sign-in, signed-in sessions, language, and links to your WorkEasy360 plan and wallets. Open it from the account menu (your name at the top right) and choose **My Account**. Business settings are in **Settings**, not here.

## Changing your password

1. Open **My Account** (`/account`).
2. Under **Change Password**, enter your **Current password**.
3. Enter a **New password** (at least 8 characters, with an uppercase letter and a number) and repeat it in **Confirm new password**.
4. Click **Update Password**.

Your other sessions are signed out. Forgot the current password? Sign out and use **Forgot Password?** on the sign-in page.

## Turning on two-factor authentication

1. Open **My Account** (`/account`) and find **Two-Factor Authentication**.
2. Click **Set up two-factor sign-in** and confirm your **Current password**.
3. Open an authenticator app on your phone (for example Google Authenticator, Microsoft Authenticator or 1Password) and scan the QR code, or enter the key by hand.
4. Type the **6-digit code** the app shows and click **Turn on**.
5. Copy or download your recovery codes, then click **I have saved them**.

From then on, signing in asks for a code from the app as well as your password. A recovery code works once if you lose your phone.

## Turning off two-factor authentication or making new recovery codes

In **Two-Factor Authentication**, click **Turn off** or **New recovery codes**, then confirm your current password and a current code (or a recovery code). New recovery codes replace the old ones. If a staff member has lost their authenticator app, the owner can use **Reset two-factor sign-in** in **Settings → Team** under **Login access**.

## Active sessions on other devices

**Active Sessions** lists the browsers and devices signed in to your account, marking **This browser**. Click **Sign out other sessions** to sign out everywhere else.

## Changing the language

In **My Account** (`/account`), choose **English** or **Hindi** under **Language**. It applies right away in this browser. Customers choose their own language on the booking page.

## Changing your name or email address

Your name and sign-in email belong to your WorkEasy360 account and are shown under **Profile** in **My Account**. They cannot be changed in WorkEasy360 Booking; ask WorkEasy360 support with **Help → Create Ticket**. The business's contact email is a different setting, in **Settings → Business Details**.

## Changing your profile photo

Under **Profile** in **My Account**, upload a **Profile photo**. It is shown in the top bar. Your name, email and role are shared across WorkEasy360 products.

## Plan, billing and wallets

WorkEasy360 Booking uses your WorkEasy360 plan. **Plan & Billing** and **Wallets & Usage** (in **My Account** under **Plan & Wallets**, and in the account menu) open your WorkEasy360 account in a new tab. The Consumption Wallet pays for SMS and WhatsApp messages. Customers are never charged for booking.

## Getting help and support

The **Help** menu (question mark in the top bar) has:

- **Unified Helpdesk**: WorkEasy360 help articles.
- **Create Ticket**: ask WorkEasy360 support for help.
- **My Tickets**: follow your support requests.
- **Ask the AI Assistant**: opens this assistant. The round button at the bottom right of the screen does the same.

## Deleting your account

Deleting an account or a business yourself is not available in WorkEasy360 Booking. Ask WorkEasy360 support with **Help → Create Ticket**. To stop using Booking meanwhile, switch off **Accept online bookings** in **Settings → Public Booking**, and the owner can turn off a staff member's sign-in in **Settings → Team** with **Login access**.
