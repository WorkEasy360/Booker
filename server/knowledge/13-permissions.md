# Roles and Permissions — Guide

Everyone signs in with their own account. A sign-in role decides what a person can see and change. The owner chooses the role when inviting someone in **Settings → Team** (`/settings/team`) with **Login access**. The server checks every change, so hidden buttons cannot be bypassed.

## The roles

| Role | Who it is for | Main access |
|---|---|---|
| Owner | The business owner and anyone who manages the business | Everything |
| Staff | People who take appointments | Their own appointments, view only |
| Manager | Accounts invited as Manager before (no longer offered when inviting) | Appointments, customers, services and online booking |

## What an owner can do

The owner (shown as Owner; the account that created the business) can do everything: all appointments, customers, availability for the business and every staff member, every Settings section (Business Details, Services, Notifications, Public Booking, Team, Internal Automation, Integrations), inviting and removing sign-in access, and the AI Assistant.

## What a staff member can do

A staff member can see only their own appointments and their own working hours and blocked dates (view only), not all appointments. Staff cannot create, change or cancel appointments, cannot see the Customers list, cannot change availability or settings, and cannot use the AI Assistant. In Settings they see **Business settings are managed by your owner**. They manage their own password, two-factor sign-in and language in **My Account**.

## What a manager can do

The Manager role is kept for people invited as managers earlier. A manager can see and manage every appointment (create, edit, reschedule, cancel, complete, no-show), add and edit customers, manage **Settings → Services**, switch **Accept online bookings** in **Settings → Public Booking**, and use the AI Assistant. A manager cannot add, edit or deactivate staff members or invite them, cannot change availability, and cannot change Business Details, Notifications, Internal Automation, Integrations or the change cutoff.

## Changing a staff member's role

The role is chosen in the invitation. While an invitation is still pending, open **Login access** in **Settings → Team** (`/settings/team`), choose another **Sign-in role** and click **Send a new invitation**. Changing the role of someone who already signs in is not available in the app; you can turn off their sign-in with **Turn off sign-in**.

## The AI Assistant and permissions

The AI Assistant is available to owners and managers. It only sees the signed-in business's data, and it can only prepare a change the person is allowed to make. Nothing is saved until they press **Confirm**.
