# Settings — Guide

Settings (`/settings`) keeps every business setting in one place. Its sections are **Business Details**, **Services**, **Notifications**, **Public Booking**, **Team**, **Internal Automation** and **Integrations**. Each person sees only the sections their role can use. Your own password, two-factor sign-in and language are not here: they are in **My Account**.

## Settings sections

| Section | Address | What it is for |
|---|---|---|
| Business Details | `/settings/business` | Business name, contact email, phone, timezone and appointment location |
| Services | `/settings/services` | What customers can book and how long it lasts |
| Notifications | `/settings/notifications` | Email, WhatsApp and SMS channels and the 24-hour reminder |
| Public Booking | `/settings/public-booking` | Accept online bookings, your booking link and the change cutoff |
| Team | `/settings/team` | Staff members, their services and sign-in access |
| Internal Automation | `/settings/automation` | The built-in booking messages, shown read-only |
| Integrations | `/settings/integrations` | WorkEasy Connect, webhooks and ready-made connectors |

Working hours and holidays are not in Settings: they are in **Availability**.

## Changing business details

1. Go to **Settings → Business Details** (`/settings/business`).
2. Change the **Business name**, **Contact email** or **Phone**.
3. Optionally fill in **Appointment location or online instructions (optional)**, for example the address or how the video link is sent.
4. Click **Save Changes**.

The business name and contact details appear on your booking page, emails and reminders. Customers see the location before they confirm a booking, and after the change cutoff they see your contact details.

## Changing the business timezone

1. Go to **Settings → Business Details** (`/settings/business`).
2. Choose the new **Timezone** (time zone).
3. Click **Save Changes**.
4. In **Change the business timezone?**, click **Change timezone**.

What happens then: times are shown in the new timezone. Existing appointments are not moved: each keeps its exact moment in time, so its local time may display differently. No customer is notified. The business has one timezone, used by the booking page, reminders and every appointment.

## Setting the appointment location or address

The **Appointment location or online instructions (optional)** field in **Settings → Business Details** (`/settings/business`) tells customers where to come, such as your shop, salon or clinic address, or how an online appointment works (for example: we will send a video link before your appointment). Customers see it before they confirm a booking and on their confirmation. Add or change it there and click **Save Changes**.

## Settings for staff

Staff who open Settings see **Business settings are managed by your owner**, with **Open My Account** for their own password, two-factor sign-in and language. Managers see Services and Public Booking.

## Currency and prices

WorkEasy360 Booking does not use prices or a currency: services have no price, customers never pay online and there is no revenue reporting. Customers pay the business directly.
