# Availability — Guide

Availability (`/availability`) is where you set when the business is open and when each staff member takes appointments, including breaks, holidays, time off and special hours. Customers and your team can only book times that fit inside it.

## How business hours and staff hours work together

A time can be booked only when it is inside both the staff member's working hours and the business working hours, and is not in a break, a blocked date or another appointment. A day marked Closed in the business hours is closed for everyone. Staff hours must fall within the business hours. Until business hours are saved, staff hours alone decide (the page says **No business hours are saved yet**).

A new team member starts with Monday to Friday, 09:00 to 17:00. Change it in Availability.

## Setting business working hours

1. Open **Availability** (`/availability`).
2. In **Showing**, choose **Business hours**.
3. Under **Business Working Hours**, switch each day **Open** or **Closed**.
4. Set the start and end time of each open day.
5. Click **Save Working Hours**.

**Unsaved changes** shows until you save. Saving never moves or cancels existing appointments.

## Setting a staff member's working hours

1. Open **Availability** (`/availability`).
2. In **Showing**, choose the staff member.
3. Under their **Working Hours**, switch each day **Open** or **Closed** and set the times.
4. Click **Save Working Hours**.

The staff member's hours can only narrow the business hours, never extend them.

## Adding a lunch break

1. Open **Availability** and choose **Business hours** or the staff member in **Showing**.
2. On the day, click **Add time range**.
3. Set two ranges with a gap between them, for example 09:00 to 13:00 and 14:00 to 18:00.
4. Click **Save Working Hours**.

Gaps between ranges are breaks and cannot be booked.

## Blocking a holiday or closing the business for a day

1. Open **Availability** and choose **Business hours** in **Showing**.
2. Under **Date Exceptions**, click **Add Exception**.
3. In **Exception type**, choose **Block full day**.
4. Choose the **From date** and **To date**, and optionally a **Reason** such as Public holiday.
5. Click **Add Exception**.

A business exception applies to the whole business and every staff member. It shows as **Closed** in the list.

## Adding time off for a staff member

1. Open **Availability** and choose the staff member in **Showing**.
2. Under **Date Exceptions**, click **Add Exception**.
3. Choose **Block full day** for days off, or **Block time period** for a few hours.
4. Choose the dates or the **Start time** and **End time**, and optionally a **Reason**.
5. Click **Add Exception**.

Time off is enforced when booking: no one can book that staff member during it, online or in the app.

## Setting different hours for one date

1. Open **Availability** and choose the business or the staff member in **Showing**.
2. Under **Date Exceptions**, click **Add Exception**.
3. Choose **Different hours** and pick the **Date**.
4. Set the **Start time** and **End time**.
5. Click **Add Exception**.

These hours replace the usual weekly hours on this date only. The list shows them as **Special hours**.

## Removing a date exception

Click the remove button next to the exception in the **Date Exceptions** list. A whole-business exception shown on a staff member's page says **Applies to the whole business**; remove it from **Business hours** instead.

## Appointments outside the new availability

Changing hours or adding an exception never changes existing appointments. When future appointments no longer fit, Availability shows **N future appointments outside the current availability** with a link to each one. Open each appointment to reschedule or cancel it, and let the customer know.

## Who can change availability

The owner can change business hours, staff hours and date exceptions. Staff can open Availability to see their own working hours and blocked dates, marked **View only**.
