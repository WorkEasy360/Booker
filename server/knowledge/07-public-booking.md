# Public Booking — Guide

Every business has one booking link: a public page where customers book without an account or login. It is managed in **Settings → Public Booking** (`/settings/public-booking`) and shown on the **Booking Link** card on Appointments. There is one booking page per business; separate pages per service or per staff member are not available.

## Sharing your booking link

1. Go to **Appointments** (`/appointments`) or **Settings → Public Booking**.
2. Click the copy button next to the **Booking link**.
3. Paste the link in WhatsApp, email, your website or social media.

Click **Preview Booking Page** to open it the way customers see it.

## Creating a booking page

There is nothing to create: your booking page is made with your account and always lists your active services that have at least one active staff member. To start taking online bookings:

1. Add at least one service in **Settings → Services**.
2. Check your working hours in **Availability**.
3. Make sure **Accept online bookings** is on in **Settings → Public Booking**.
4. Copy the **Booking link** and share it.

## Turning online booking on or off

1. Go to **Settings → Public Booking** (`/settings/public-booking`).
2. Switch **Accept online bookings** on or off. It saves at once.

When it is off, customers who open the link see **Booking page not found** and cannot book, and the Booking Link card on Appointments says **Online booking is off**. Customers can still manage existing bookings, and you can still create appointments yourself in the app.

## What customers see when they book

1. **Select a Service** (skipped when there is only one).
2. **Choose a Provider**, only when more than one person offers the service, or **Any available staff**.
3. **Select a Date**. Only days with free appointment times can be selected.
4. **Select a Time**, grouped into Morning and Afternoon.
5. **Your Information**: Full Name, plus a phone number or an email address (at least one). They can also ask for booking updates by SMS or WhatsApp.
6. **Review Your Booking**, then **Confirm Booking**.

The confirmation page shows the booking reference, date, time, timezone and location, with **Add to calendar** buttons for Google Calendar, Outlook and Apple Calendar. Customers never pay online: the page says to pay the business directly. The page is available in English and Hindi.

## The booking reference

Every appointment gets a booking reference such as WE-1A2B3C4D. It is on the confirmation page, in the booking emails and on the appointment in the app. Customers use it with the email address they booked with to find their booking, and the search on Appointments finds an appointment by it.

## How customers reschedule or cancel their booking

The confirmation email has a secure link to manage the booking. Customers without it can open **Find an existing booking** on your booking page, enter the **Booking reference** and the **Email address** they booked with, and click **Find my booking**. **Lost the email? Email me a link to manage my booking** sends the link again. They can then **Reschedule** or **Cancel Booking**.

## Setting the cutoff for customer changes

1. Go to **Settings → Public Booking** (`/settings/public-booking`).
2. Under **Customer Rescheduling / Cancellation**, choose **Allow changes until**: 1, 2, 4, 12, 24, 48 or 72 hours before the appointment. The default is 24 hours.
3. Click **Save Changes**.

After the cutoff, the customer sees **Online changes are closed for this booking** with your business contact details instead, so they call or email you. Once an appointment has started it cannot be changed online at all.

## How far ahead customers can book

The booking link offers days up to 30 days ahead, and only times that are free in Availability. Past times are never offered. You can book further ahead yourself with **New Appointment**.

## Who can change public booking settings

The owner and managers can switch **Accept online bookings**. Only the owner can change the cutoff.
